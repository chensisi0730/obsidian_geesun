## 什么是机器人仿真软件？

机器人仿真软件通常包含多个模块，这些模块协同工作，用于测试、验证和优化机器人系统的设计和控制算法。

通常机器人仿真软件包含物理引擎、图形渲染引擎、 [脚本与插件系统](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=242096677&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E8%84%9A%E6%9C%AC%E4%B8%8E%E6%8F%92%E4%BB%B6%E7%B3%BB%E7%BB%9F&zhida_source=entity) 、用户界面等多个模块。

![](https://pic2.zhimg.com/v2-562e81c91cf4ba82270fb02fe5d8a52f_1440w.jpg)

机器人仿真软件的结构

机器人仿真软件中最为重要的模块是物理引擎，它负责模拟和计算模型的物理行为，使得仿真环境更加真实和可靠。

> **MuJoCo** stands for **Mu** lti- **Jo** int dynamics with **Co** ntact. It is a general purpose physics engine that aims to facilitate research and development in robotics, biomechanics, graphics and animation, machine learning, and other areas that demand fast and accurate simulation of articulated structures interacting with their environment. Initially developed by Roboti LLC, it was acquired and made [freely available](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco/blob/main/LICENSE) by DeepMind in October 2021, and open sourced in May 2022. ![](https://pic3.zhimg.com/v2-322a823568ba42927201f5316a8c4c10_1440w.jpg)
